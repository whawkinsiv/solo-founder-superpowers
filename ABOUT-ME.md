## Opinions
- Solo founders who build 5+ products aren't unfocused — they're problem-solvers who can't
  unsee problems. The disease is real but it's also the superpower.
- Marketplace models are solo founder traps — they look like products but they're full-time
  operations jobs. Automated SaaS is the way out.
- Directory submission services are a black box — they won't tell you where, how, or whether
  it even helps. The only way to know what works is to do it yourself and check Ahrefs.
- Most distribution advice is theory. Nobody runs the actual experiments. You have to.
- The no-code ceiling is real — Bubble gets you to revenue, but rebuilding on a real stack
  is inevitable if you want to scale.

## Stories & Lessons
- **bootstrapped.app to $380k:** Built a matching marketplace solo on Bubble. Proved the
  model works but learned it doesn't scale without becoming your full-time job. Now rebuilding
  on Replit and shifting to automated SaaS.
- **Startup that crashed:** Early no-code era. Taught him what happens when you build without
  a sustainable model. Shaped the bias toward shipping revenue-generating products fast.
- **100+ manual directory submissions:** DR jumps early but plateaus. Most directories are
  spam. Paid services hide everything. Lesson: the only trustworthy distribution data is
  first-party.
- **The plumber rate card:** Clients who "help" cost more than clients who get out of the way.
  Real insight disguised as a joke.
